SCENE 08 — DIRECTOR'S NOTES / Q&A
Will my ad look “AI-generated”?+

That fear is exactly why direction matters. Every shot is storyboarded, lensed, lit and graded like a real film — then curated and re-generated until nothing feels synthetic. The goal isn't to hide the tool; it's to make people feel something before they even wonder how it was made.

Can I use the ads commercially?+

Yes — full commercial license. The master files are yours to run on paid media, socials, marketplaces, TV or anywhere else, with no extra usage fees. I only keep the right to show the work in my portfolio (and I'll ask first if the project is under embargo).

How do revisions work?+

Launch includes one revision round, Campaign two, and Retainer gets unlimited hook re-cuts. A “round” means one consolidated list of changes — edit, pacing, text, music. Changing the entire concept after approval counts as a new brief, and I'll always tell you before anything costs extra.

How fast is delivery?+

A single spot ships in about 3 days from an approved script; a 3-spot campaign in about 7. The clock starts once the brief and references are locked. Need it faster? Rush slots exist — mention your deadline in the first email.

What formats and ratios do I receive?+

Every delivery includes 9:16 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts as the hero format, plus 1:1 and 16:9 masters where the package includes them. Files come as clean H.264 MP4s (and ProRes on request), color-graded, mixed, with and without burned-in captions.
